Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with.

This is a plain-language summary of how IRON CLOUD MC treats your data. An effective date is {{effective date}}.

What we collect

  • Your Minecraft username and email address, when you make a purchase.
  • Payment details you give us in the payment form (transaction ID, sender number).
  • Your purchase history, so we can deliver items and answer your questions.

How we use it

  • To deliver ranks, coins, and crate keys after payment verification.
  • To contact you about your order or a technical issue.
  • To run the server and keep the community safe.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not use your email for third-party mailing lists.
  • We do not share your details with anyone who is not part of IRON CLOUD MC.

How to reach us

If you have questions or want your data deleted, email us at ironcloudmc@gmail.com or message us on Discord.
