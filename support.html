Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive my purchase?
All purchases are delivered after payment confirmation. If you do not receive your items within 15 minutes, please contact support.

We currently accept Bkash and Nagad for Bangladesh users. International payment methods are coming soon.
Can I get a refund?
Refunds are generally not provided for digital purchases. If you did not receive your items due to a technical error, we make sure you get what you paid for.

Yes! Our server supports both Minecraft Java Edition and Bedrock Edition players.

We support versions from 1.8 to the latest version, 1.21.8.
