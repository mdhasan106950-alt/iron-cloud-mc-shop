Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about joining, purchasing, and playing.

How do I join the server?
Add play.ironcloudmc.fun in Minecraft and hit Join. The server is free to join. No app or account is needed.
Do you support Java and Bedrock?
Yes. Both Java and Bedrock Edition players join the same server.
What versions do you support?
Versions 1.8 to the latest 1.21.8. Older versions are not supported.
How do I buy ranks, coins, or crate keys?
Choose a package in the store, then complete the payment on the payment page. Ranks, coins, and keys are delivered after payment verification.
How long does delivery take?
Items are delivered after payment verification, usually within 15 minutes. If you do not receive your items, contact the Discord support team.
What payment methods do you accept?
Bkash and Nagad. International methods are coming soon.
Can I get a refund?
Digital purchases are generally not refunded. If you did not receive items because of a technical error, we make sure you get what you paid for.
Where can I get help?
Join the Discord community for fast help, or email ironcloudmc@gmail.com. Staff respond during business hours.
